## 1. Branding & Basic Information
- [ ] **Hospital Logo:** High-resolution PNG or vector file (transparent background).
- [ ] **Contact Details:** Official Phone Numbers, WhatsApp Number, and Email ID for receiving appointment requests.
- [ ] **Location:** Full Hospital Address and a Google Maps Location Link.
- [ ] **Timings:** Standard OPD Working Hours and Days.

## 2. Doctors Information (For each Doctor)
- [ ] **Profile Photo:** Clear, professional photograph.
- [ ] **Details:** Full Name, Qualifications, and Speciality (e.g., General Surgeon, 15 Years Exp).
- [ ] **Consultation Timings:** 
   - In-person OPD Timings.
   - Dedicated Online Consultation Timings (e.g., 12:00 PM - 12:30 PM).

## 3. Departments & Facilities (Content & Photos)
- [ ] **Departments:** A list of departments (e.g., Cardiology, Paediatrics) along with a short 2-3 line description for each.
- [ ] **Facilities:** A list of hospital facilities (e.g., 24/7 Pharmacy, Advanced ICU, Modular OT) with relevant real photographs.

## 4. Home Page & About Us
- [ ] **Hospital Photography:** Real, high-quality photos of the Hospital exterior, Reception, Wards, etc., for the Home Page sliders.
- [ ] **Trust & Milestones:** Key verified numbers to highlight (e.g., "10+ Years of Trust", "10,000+ Successful Surgeries").
- [ ] **About Content:** A short paragraph about the Hospital's history, Vision, and Mission.

## 5. Technical Requirements (If applicable)
- [ ] **WhatsApp API:** Any API Documentation, access keys, or vendor contact details for the WhatsApp integration you mentioned.
